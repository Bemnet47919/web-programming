Welcome to Our Kitchen

At Habesha Table Restaurant, we bring families and friends together around a traditional mesob. Every dish is prepared fresh daily using authentic spices, organic teff injera, and time-honored recipes passed down through generations.

Why Dine With Us

  • 100% pure red and white teff injera baked fresh every morning
  • Traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony served after every meal
  • Rich vegetarian and fasting platters available every day of the week
  • Warm hospitality and live cultural music on Friday and Saturday nights
